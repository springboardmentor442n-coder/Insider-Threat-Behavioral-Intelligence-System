Methodology

Unlike threshold-based labeling approaches, ground-truth labels in this pipeline were matched at the individual log-row level against CERT's official answer files — ensuring the model learns genuine behavioral patterns rather than re-deriving arbitrary rules. The train/test split is performed by user identity (not by date) to prevent identity leakage, giving an honest estimate of how the model performs on employees it has never seen before.
